# Project working preferences

- The user is Member A. Complete Member A's work one module at a time and give a short handover with the actions the user should try after a module is finished.
- The application uses the existing Firebase project `themeparksmartmanagement` and its registered accounts and data. Do not introduce a separate demo or testing application, or switch the running app to local Firebase services.
- Use normal application wording. Keep demo, prototype and development notices out of the interface.
- Follow the FYP Figma UI design for Member A's screens: https://www.figma.com/design/GJ5FB6hMeMkERMfJ8NHftA/FYP?node-id=0-1 . Inspect the relevant frames and reuse the established assets, colours and layout.
- Reproduce the relevant Figma screens and their navigation faithfully. Do not substitute a simplified form or merge designed screens. UI alignment is part of module completion.
- Member B will bring their modules here for integration. Keep unrelated Member B features out of the current Member A module.
- Figma is the reference for UI style and navigation, not fixed account data. Use current Firebase names, dates, balances and bookings. Add missing UI states or flow steps when needed to make a module work.
- The ticket payment action confirms and saves the booking without a bank/card charge, as agreed. Do not persist card numbers, expiry dates or CVVs.
- Do not push to GitHub, merge or change the Firebase billing plan unless the user requests it.
- Automated checks may use isolated fixtures; do not overwrite the user's existing visitor records or create fixture accounts in the shared Firebase project.
