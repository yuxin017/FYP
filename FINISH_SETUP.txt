LIVE ATTRACTION CAMERAS
The camera feature needs its Firestore access rules published before use.
1. On the PC, run .\tool\run-app.cmd -Restart and open the Admin Portal.
2. Go to Crowd Monitoring -> Live Cameras. Select each attraction and click
   Assign Camera. Only assigned attractions are available on the camera phones.
3. Plug each Android phone into the PC once to install the camera build:
     .\tool\run-android.cmd -Camera
   For multiple connected phones, add -DeviceId "SERIAL".
4. Unplug the phones and open Theme Park on each. Join the same private Wi-Fi
   or hotspot as the PC. Sign in using an existing verified administrator
   account, select the assigned attraction, then tap Start Camera. Keep the
   screen open and use a fixed view of the queue.
5. On the PC, click View Camera on each online tile. Use Heatmap to show or hide
   the overlay. Move between the camera areas and compare detections to a manual
   count. Stop a camera and check that its feed closes.

The phone detects people locally. The PC receives video and aggregate heat
over WebRTC. Firebase pairs the devices and tracks whether a camera is online.
Keep internet access for Firebase and a Wi-Fi network that allows device-to-device
connections. The viewer supports one open browser per attraction camera.
