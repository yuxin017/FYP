WHERE TO FIND THE COMBINED FEATURES
- Home: Tickets, Virtual Queue, My Groups, My Park Photos, Park Offers, Assistant.
- Profile: combined Purchase History, Visited Attractions, Points & Vouchers,
  personal details, preferences, security, linked phone and photo matching.
- Map: park search, landmark camera, directions, assistant and configured venue
  navigation. Group live location is opt-in and stops when you leave its screen.
- Admin: visitors, orders, packages, photos, promotions/rewards, reports,
  landmarks, crowd monitoring, unusual activity, facilities, reservations,
  admissions and support.
- Kiosk: collect a paid print order, or start a separate visitor shopping session.

TRY THESE FLOWS YOURSELF
1. Sign in with your existing visitor account. Check name, balance and profile.
2. Buy a pass for a valid visit day; add each guest, review points and confirm.
   Reopen My Tickets and Purchase History; open the QR/NFC pass and receipt.
3. Reserve an open attraction with a valid ticket and eligible height. Check the
   return window, reminder, cancellation and entrance pass.
4. Create a group, add/join members, link separate tickets and reserve together.
   Test a guest pass as well as a registered member. Share/stop live location.
5. In admin Attraction Check-in, scan/enter the reservation during its window.
   Check points once, then reopen the completed visit and submit a rating.
6. Optional photo matching: enrol your own clear selfie with consent. In admin
   Smart Photos, upload an actual park photo with capture time/place. You can
   also use the photographer's photo code without enrolling a face profile.
7. Preview/edit a matched photo; try crop, rotation, filters, brightness, beauty
   and stickers. Choose digital/print/bundle, then confirm the purchase.
8. Reopen the photo order, download a digital copy/receipt, and open its kiosk
   collection pass. Test print preview and cancellation. Confirm a printer job
   only when sent; mark collected only after the physical copies are handed over.
9. In the kiosk, try Browse & Purchase Photos with a visitor account. End the
   session and confirm the next visitor cannot see the previous visitor's cart.
10. Create a promotion/reward in admin. Check its visibility, dates, redemption
    limit and points-combination rule. Test a ticket/photo promotion code.
11. Generate a report for a date range, check totals/top products and export PDF.
    Review voucher issuance/use and redemption rates. In Crowd Monitoring, try
    the period filters, heatmap selection, demand search and Manage Facilities.
12. Ask the assistant questions in English, Malay and Chinese. Try its feature
    links, voice input and read-aloud button where your browser/device supports it.
13. Check device sessions, password recovery, linked phone and device unlock.
    Do not delete your account merely to review the application.
