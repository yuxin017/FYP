TRY THESE IN ORDER
1. Profile: open Points. Check your actual balance, reward membership progress,
   monthly totals and All/Earned/Redeemed filters. Open the ? help button.
   Names, points and dates are your Firebase data, not the values in Figma.

2. If a completed purchase has uncollected points, press Collect Points.
   Your existing RM140 purchase should offer 140 points if still uncollected.
   Refresh and reopen: the points stay saved and the purchase cannot earn twice.
   No visitor balances were manually filled for this module.

3. Make a new ticket booking normally. Earn 1 point per full RM1 of the final
   ticket total after any points discount. A RM140 purchase earns 140 points.
   New purchases collect automatically. If that follow-up is interrupted,
   Points & History offers the uncollected points without rebooking.

4. At ticket Review Order, try the existing points discount: 100 points = RM1.
   Check the discounted total and resulting balance. The earned points are
   based on the discounted total; RM139 earns 139 points. Booking history and
   QR tickets should still work normally.

5. Open View Redemption Catalogue. Try All/Dining/Shopping/Photos. Review a
   reward and expand its terms. Insufficient points disables redemption.
   Current rewards cost 150, 200 and 300 points; earn enough before redeeming.
   Confirm one reward: the exact cost is deducted once and one voucher appears.

6. Profile > Vouchers: check the available count, open the new voucher and
   inspect its QR, copy-code button, expiry, How to use and Terms & Conditions.
   Refresh the app and reopen it: the voucher is still saved. Vouchers expire
   at the end of their last valid day in Malaysia time, 30 days including issue day.

7. Press Use Voucher, then Not Yet: it should remain Available. Press it again
   and Confirm Use: it should move to Used, reduce the available count and
   disable further use. Refresh and check again. Confirm Use consumes the voucher.
   Dining/shopping/photo benefits are presented to staff at the relevant counter.
   This module records the reward and its use; staff handle counter fulfilment.

8. Return to Points & History. The redemption is listed, the monthly redeemed
   total includes it, and Earned/Redeemed filters separate the activity.
   Rewards membership uses lifetime earned points and does not drop when spent:
   Normal below 500; Silver 500; Gold 1,500; Platinum 3,000.
