TRY THIS BOOKING
1. Open Tickets > Buy Tickets > choose 1-Day Pass.
2. Choose a visit date. Set Adult=1, Child=1: total RM250.
   Adult=2, Child=0 should total RM280. Up to 10 guests per order.
3. Add to Cart. Try Edit, change counts, and remove a pass.
   Add another pass if desired; its date and guest counts stay separate.
4. Checkout. Enter each guest's name and email. Try Assign this ticket to me.
   Continue stays disabled until every guest has valid details.
5. Review Order. Check dates, fares and total. A zero points balance correctly
   disables points redemption. Review the ticket terms and tick the agreement.
6. Proceed to Payment. Choose a method and press Pay once.
   You do not need to enter real card/bank details. As agreed, this confirms
   and saves the booking without making a bank/card charge.
7. Expect Payment Successful. Open View Digital Tickets: one distinct QR per
   guest. Download Receipt and check its booking reference, dates and total.
8. Open My Tickets and Purchase History. Refresh/sign in again: confirmed
   tickets and the order remain. Add to Wallet saves an in-app bookmark.
9. Try Scan & Add. Enter your ticket ID, or allow the camera and scan its QR.
   A ticket must belong to you or be assigned to your verified account email.
   Random, expired, used or another account's ticket should not be accepted.
10. Leave a cart unfinished, refresh, and confirm it restores for your account.
    If a connection fails during Pay, Retry checkout reuses the same booking
    reference. Review/change first checks Firebase for an existing confirmation.

CURRENT DEVICE BEHAVIOUR
- Chrome/web is the checked target. Camera scanning requires camera permission
  and an available camera. Manual ticket ID lookup is also available.
- NFC has a QR fallback on this device. Add to Wallet is the in-app wallet,
  not an Apple/Google Wallet pass. Browser screen brightness is controlled
  through your device's display controls; the app offers a brighter QR view.
- Souvenir cards show gift-shop information; merchandise checkout and gate
  ticket redemption are outside this ticket-booking module.
