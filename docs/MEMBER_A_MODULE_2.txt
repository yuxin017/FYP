READY TO TRY
1. Sign in to Admin Portal. A normal visitor account must not get admin access.
   The Figma reference is the desktop Admin Portal frame 128:529.

2. Visitors: search by name/email/phone/visitor ID; try status, membership, risk
   and joined-date filters. Click Visitor/Membership/Last Activity headings to
   sort. Select rows and try pagination or Export CSV.
   Figma reference: Visitor Accounts, frame 135:551.

3. Open a visitor. Check the overview, loyalty balance, current tickets,
   activity, payments and access details. Edit the name/phone/membership/risk
   tag, or save an admin note. Reopen/refresh to check that it persists.
   Admin notes are private to administrators. Figma profile frame: 135:552.

4. Pass Packages: add or edit a pass. Adult and child fares use exact RM/sen
   amounts. Set its category, validity days, display order and availability.
   Open the visitor catalog in the other tab: the change should appear live.
   Inactive passes disappear from new-booking choices. Existing bookings keep
   their original price/date. Restore any fare/availability you change while
   checking. A cart with an old fare may need that pass removed and re-added.

5. Tickets & Orders: inspect actual booking records, individual tickets and
   receipts. Search a booking/ticket/guest, switch tabs, filter ticket status,
   open a QR and download a receipt or CSV.

6. Ticket Validation: scan a ticket QR or paste its readable ticket ID/full QR
   value. First check the guest, dates and status. Press Admit Visitor once.
   A current valid ticket should confirm admission; a second scan must not
   admit it twice. Future, expired, used or cancelled tickets must be rejected.
   Single-day tickets move to Used. Multi-day/annual passes allow one admission
   per valid day, retaining their remaining validity. The visitor's ticket view
   updates from Firebase. Camera scanning needs an available camera/permission;
   manual entry also works.

7. Account actions: suspend a visitor, then check that their visitor app shows
   the access notice. Activate them to restore access. Remove archives the
   account without deleting its profile/bookings; restore it through the
   Archived status filter. You cannot restrict your own administrator account.
   These actions restrict visitor-app access and new bookings; they do not
   delete the Firebase Auth account or cancel previously issued tickets.

8. Activity Monitoring: check real visitor sessions, admin actions and successful
   park admissions. Try the activity filter, Live updates switch and CSV export.
   Dashboard counts use the current data. Watchlist/risk tags are admin-managed;
   they are not an automated risk-detection result.

DESIGN
Original Figma artwork is reused. Visitor list/profile follow the original
rose sidebar, header, stat cards, filters, tables and detail layout. Package,
ticket and validation screens extend that style where the design has gaps.
Desktop is the main target; smaller windows use a drawer and scrollable tables.
This module does not add the separate administrator-role, automated-risk,
complaint-handling or report-generation workflows.
