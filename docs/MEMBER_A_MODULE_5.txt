TRY THESE
1. Profile Settings: check your real name, avatar and completion percentage.
   Personal Information: edit name/phone/date/gender, then Save Changes. Refresh
   and verify persistence. Try an invalid phone number and Cancel in its editor.
   Change or remove your avatar. Unsaved changes ask before you leave.

2. Preferences & Interests: enter a nickname, adjust height with the slider or
   number input, choose foods and interests, then Save Preferences. Reopen the
   page to confirm. All food preferences is exclusive. Height may be cleared.
   Your nickname appears in the Home greeting; full name remains on your profile.

3. Personal Information > edit Email Address: enter the new address and current
   password. Open the real verification link in the new inbox, return and press
   I Have Verified My Email. Until verification succeeds, the old email remains.
   Changing email affects how you next sign in. This action is optional to test.

4. Security & Account > Reset Password: try mismatched confirmation, then change
   the password if you want to. The current password is required. No passwords
   were changed by the agent. Your existing credentials continue to work.

5. From Log In > Forgot Password: request a reset email. Open its Firebase link,
   or paste that link into the app's Check Your Email screen to enter a new
   password in the application's reset form. Expired/used links are rejected.

6. Log in from a second browser/window. Login & Security lists recorded devices
   and last-active times; the current device is labelled. Sign Out All Devices
   asks for your password and invalidates earlier sessions, including this one.
   Reconnect the other window: it should return to sign-in. A new sign-in works.

7. At login, Remember me checked persists across browser restarts. Unchecked
   uses session persistence and ends when that browser session closes.

8. Delete Account: inspect the full screen and choose Keep My Account on your
   main account. Final deletion requires the checkbox, exact DELETE text and
   current password. Only confirm on an account you intend to erase. Deletion
   really removes owned tickets, rewards, support data and the Auth account.
   An active attraction reservation must be cancelled first. Interrupted cleanup
   can be resumed by reopening the app and confirming the password again.
